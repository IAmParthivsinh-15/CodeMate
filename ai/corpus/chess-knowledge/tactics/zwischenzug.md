# Zwischenzug

## Definition
A zwischenzug (German for "in-between move", also called an intermezzo) is an unexpected move inserted into a sequence that seems forced. The most common case is during an exchange: your opponent captures a piece and expects you to recapture at once, but instead you first play a more forcing move, such as a check, a capture or a strong threat, and only recapture afterwards. Because the opponent must answer the in-between move, you gain something extra: a tempo, a better square for your piece, or additional material.

## How a zwischenzug works
A zwischenzug works because "obvious" recaptures are not always compulsory. Suppose your opponent takes one of your knights, and you could take back right away. Before doing so, ask whether you have a check or a capture that the opponent must respond to. If the piece that just captured cannot escape anyway, you can often play the forcing move first and still recapture on the next move. A typical case: your opponent's capturing piece is trapped or pinned, and you first give check, win a pawn with check, or attack their queen, and only then collect the piece. The net result is that you gain the extra move for free.

## Common zwischenzug situations
Look for a zwischenzug in these moments:
- During an exchange of pieces, when you have a check available before recapturing.
- When your opponent attacks one of your pieces and you can counter-attack a more valuable piece of theirs first.
- When your queen is attacked and you can move it with check or with a capture instead of simply retreating.
- When your opponent's "forced" line relies on you recapturing a particular way.
Your opponent can also use a zwischenzug against you, which is why long calculated lines must be checked for in-between checks at every step.

## How to spot it in your games
Before making an automatic recapture, pause for a moment and ask: do I have any check, capture or big threat first? And when you calculate an exchange you have started, ask the same question from your opponent's side: after my capture, must they recapture, or can they insert a check or attack my queen first? Many calculation errors come from assuming the opponent will simply take back. A useful habit is to scan for forcing moves at every step of a sequence, not just at the start.

## Key takeaways
A zwischenzug is an in-between move that interrupts an expected sequence. It usually is a check, capture or strong threat. It works when the original recapture will still be available afterwards. Always check for forcing moves before recapturing, and when calculating, assume your opponent may also play an intermezzo instead of the obvious reply. The in-between move must be at least as forcing as the threat it ignores; otherwise your opponent simply keeps the material.
